# great-citte-bases# 

🌙 Mindful Vibe-Checking (Chittanupassana)

### *จิตตานุปัสสนาสติปัฏฐาน ฉบับวัยรุ่นสำรวม*

A digital sanctuary designed to help you observe your current "mood" or "vibe" with clarity and simplicity. This project implements a dual-language toggle (Thai/English) wrapped in a **Dark Night & Deep Gold** aesthetic, reflecting a state of calm, artisan mindfulness.

---

## 🕊️ Concept: "Just Knowing"
การฝึกสังเกตใจตามหลัก **จิตตานุปัสสนา** ไม่ใช่การบังคับใจ แต่คือการ **"แค่รู้ว่าตอนนี้ใจเราเป็นยังไง"** มองโลกและมู้ดของตัวเองตามความเป็นจริง ไม่ตัดสิน ไม่ฝืน แต่เฝ้าดูอย่างสำรวมและสงบ

- **Vibe:** Cool, Chilled out, and Serene.
- **Visuals:** Minimalist interface with high-aesthetic Deep Gold accents on a dark void background.
- **Language:** A blend of modern international phrasing and polite, humble Thai.

## ✨ Key Features
- **Dual-Language Support:** Seamlessly toggle between Thai and English.
- **16 States of Mind:** A curated guide to checking your internal vibes, from "Focus Mode" to "Everyday Mode."
- **Aesthetic Design:** Optimized for a "Dark Night" viewing experience, easy on the eyes and the mind.

## 🛠️ How to Use
1. Open `index.html` in your browser.
2. Use the **TH / EN** button to switch to your preferred language.
3. Breathe, observe, and simply acknowledge your current state.

- **Watch Yourself:** Observe your own mind and feelings.
- **Learn from Others:** Cultivate empathy by understanding the vibes of those around you.
- **Embrace Impermanence:** Watch how feelings arise, stay, and eventually fade away.
